## IHG evidence and changes

- Modern search requests can have `geoLocation: null` and an explicit hotel list.
  Search identity now includes dates, hotels, guests, currency/options; old
  rewards responses are rejected before matching hotel IDs.
- The current hotel quick view puts price in
  `.ihg-ui-hotel-quick-view [data-testid="rate-details"]`, outside the scrollable
  `.p-dialog-content`. Hotel links identify the correct mnemonic.
- Four-night ORDHA search (October 12–16, one adult, one room) showed 113,250
  points/night and a 453,000-point stay. This was anonymous; no cardmember benefit
  was claimed or live-verified.
- Both `/find-hotels/select-roomrate` and the standalone `/ordha/hoteldetail`
  page load `rateDetails` responses with award and cash offers. The room view
  parses those responses directly and issues **zero additional hotel pricing
  requests**. `productUses[].inventoryTypeCode` matches `room-card-title-KSUG`
  and similar DOM IDs; room modals match unambiguous product names.
- Switching the live room page from Points to Money retained room-specific
  values and issued no offers requests during the observed toggle.
- Example KSUG room: cash stay $2,573.06 versus 453,000 points, with $140 in listed
  fixed award fees including tax. Estimated CPP is
  `(2573.06 - 140) / 453000 * 100 = 0.5371`, displayed as approximately 0.54¢/pt.
  The selected cash plan was the member Stay Longer & Save nonrefundable rate;
  its terms and eligibility appear in the tooltip.
- Award `offer.totalRate` is not the guest's full-points cash cost. Do not compare
  that internal amount as if it were a public cash rate. The room parser takes
  cash from a separate eligible cash offer, preferring room-only rates, uses actual award stay points,
  and subtracts explicitly listed fixed award charges. Variable charges and
  differences in rate terms make this an estimate, labeled with `≈`.
- Package-widget requests return restricted sets of offers; ignore
  `rateDetails.packagesAdditionalInfo` captures when choosing the main comparison.
- Normal search tooltips no longer include the Points + Cash row. Existing
  mixed-mode calculations were corrected but further mixed-mode work was stopped
  at the user's request.

## Request and rendering changes

- Removed the redundant IHG cash replay from the completion listener; native
  fetch/XHR captures provide the original response.
- Native `fetch` resolves before the cloned body is inspected. No global
  `Response.prototype` patch remains. Only the exact offers endpoint is captured.
- Removed shipped captured member credentials and the synthetic fallback search.
- Added an IHG request budget shared across tabs: six extra calls/minute,
  five-minute response cache, concurrent duplicate suppression, no automatic
  retries, 15-second timeout. 429 pauses for at least 15 minutes, respects longer
  `Retry-After`, and persists the cooldown in session storage. Native requests
  remain under the site's control.
- Per-hotel detail failures back off instead of triggering another request when
  the dialog updates. Late responses from a previous search are discarded.
- FX calls share caching and deduplication in the worker; failed conversion is
  shown as unavailable rather than valuing foreign currency as USD.
- Observers ignore the extension's own DOM changes. Tooltips share an accessible
  top-layer implementation so they remain visible in dialogs and via keyboard.

## Cheapest alternative fallback — current policy

The user requested that missing or incompatible counterparts fall back to the
cheapest available hotel alternative in either payment direction. This supersedes
the earlier unscored “Cash package only” behavior above.

- Keep the displayed points or cash amount. Prefer a same-room comparison; if
  unavailable or package-only, choose the cheapest eligible opposite option
  from this hotel's native response for the same stay and guests.
- On points pages prefer room-only cash across the hotel. If no room-only cash
  exists anywhere in the response, use its cheapest cash package with an explicit
  inclusions note. Cash pages keep the displayed plan and choose cheapest points.
- Label fallback badges “hotel alternative” and name both cash and points rooms
  in the tooltip. Use the fixed award charges of the selected points room.
- The 60,000-point KAJN example uses Premium Room's $340.87 cash alternative:
  (340.87 − 35) / 60000 × 100 ≈ 0.51¢/pt. Its native $487.10 cash package no
  longer overrides the available room-only fallback on points pages.
- No additional pricing requests. Tests cover both directions, changed payment
  controls, exact-plan dialogs, tax basis, currency isolation, absent inventory,
  and the all-packages case.

Live confirmation: the rebuilt extension on the exact points URL showed
“≈0.51¢/pt · $340.87/night · hotel alternative” on the classic room. The tooltip
identified Premium Room as cash and Classic City View as points. Switching to
Money retained exact-room 0.44/70,000-point and 0.43/71,000-point comparisons.
No extension-worker pricing requests were observed. The updated test browser
was returned to Points.

## Compact presentation follow-up

Room cards and dialogs now match the compact search badge style: a separate
circular info icon and one colored CPP/counterpart pill. The native page's
nightly basis applies to the badge. The full-stay tooltip uses a Cash/Points
table for base, taxes/fees, cash totals, points and value. Dates, tax basis,
rate terms and alternative room names appear in a smaller footer, replacing
the long label/value list. Search tooltips retain Lowest/Highest columns.
Cash base amounts come from native totalRate.baseAmount; missing breakdowns
show a dash rather than an invented base or fee amount.

The live example reconciled $257.25 base + $83.62 charges = $340.87 cash,
versus 60,000 points + $35 award charges. Typecheck, build, 11 unit checks
and 13 browser checks pass; browser checks include narrow-screen tooltip width.
